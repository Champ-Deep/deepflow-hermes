# Deep Flow: lineage

Deep Flow is Sreedeep "Champ" Surapaneni's passion project, started April 2026 in Bangalore. It is now Sreekar Surapaneni's to run, as a small stable that supports the flow arts community. This file explains the two earlier versions so the reasoning survives, not so they get rebuilt.

## The line that never changed

> Flow state of mind to the grind state of life.

Every version of Deep Flow has kept this promise. Flow state is what a poi spinner feels mid-arc, what a rider feels when the horse stops fighting the reins, and what a founder feels when product, team and timing click. Deep Flow is the bridge between the literal flow arts and the metaphorical flow that powers good work. It was never a wellness brand and never a productivity hack.

## Version 1: the creative arts community (April 2026)

**What it was.** A creative arts community brand across music, visual art, dance, DJ and electronic, digital art, and theater. Instagram led. Vibe described as "rooftop lounge": curated, ambient, elevated, intimate. Positioning slightly underground. Year one scale: Bangalore wide. Core philosophy: "Community over profit. RAW. ORGANIC. DEEP."

**The operating model.** AI runs everything digital, humans run only venue booking, live event operations and sponsor relations. The digital side was designed as an eight agent org on Paperclip (Flow Director, Content Engine, Social Pulse, Community Mod, Event Marketer, Email and CRM, Analytics, Design Ops) with a $350 a month total budget, heartbeats from four hours to daily, and approval gates on anything with commitments, pricing, merch, bans or sponsor mentions.

**The revenue model.** Sponsorships 33 percent, events 28, classes 20, community 10, merch 9. Sponsorship first meant the whole digital operation optimized for audience growth, because audience size drives sponsor revenue.

**What carries over.**
- The manifesto: flow states change lives; the best art combines technology and craft; events should leave people feeling connected to people who get it; the community is a movement, not a membership.
- The three human only jobs (venue, live ops, sponsor relations). Everything else is fair game for Hermes.
- The event ladder: workshops, showcases, corporate programs, retreats first; jam sessions, newbie nights, collab residencies and pop up markets later.
- The approval gate thinking: content can flow, commitments need a human.
- The superfan idea: track the people who show up three times, create content, bring friends.

**What was dropped.** The six discipline spread (music, theater, DJ) was too wide for one person and one stable. The eight agent Paperclip org was designed for a budget and a team that did not exist. See `05-decisions.md`.

## Version 2: Editorial Ranch (May 2026)

**What it was.** Deep Flow became Champ's personal brand and community concept with a physical anchor: a ranch outside Bangalore with horse rides, flow arts sessions, open fire evenings and founder dinners. Disciplines narrowed to movement arts: flow arts, equestrian, yoga, martial arts, capoeira. "Not a yoga studio. Not a gym. A gathering point."

**The visual direction, "Editorial Ranch".** A well designed ranching magazine, not a resort brochure. Cream and clay palette with deep earth tones, display serif, golden hour photography, hand drawn marks, zero wellness cliches (no mandalas, no gradient sunsets, no sans serif on beige minimalism).

**The palette, "Forest Forager".** Pine `#1F4A36` dominant, Lilac `#D7CFEC` as the surprise, Terracotta `#B95A2D` as sparing warmth, Cream `#F0E8D5` as ground, Char `#1A1A14` for text. Rule: pine holds the canvas, lilac keeps it from reading as generic outdoorsy, terracotta at most three times per view, never all five on one surface.

**Type.** GT Alpina for display (fallback Instrument Serif), Inter for body, JetBrains Mono for captions and labels. Sentence case display, no all caps in the serif, no italic in body.

**Marks.** A dingbat family instead of a logo: horse, poi, sun, leaf, asterisk, peace, smiley, sprig. Used as headline punctuation, merch marks and section dividers, never as a wordmark. A character named Dapo (a capoeira figure, "Cowboy Dabo" at the ranch) was proposed and then removed from the design system: "There is no mascot, character illustration, or Dapo concept."

**Voice.** Wry but warm. Confident from having done the thing, not from performing expertise. Allowed words: flow, presence, state, motion, grounded, edge, build, ride, craft, earned. Banned: "Submit" and "Register Now" as CTAs (use "Come in" or "Reserve your spot"), "wellness journey", "transformative", "unlock", "empower", generic startup language. Emoji: one per post at most, only if it adds meaning.

**What carries over.** The voice rules, the banned list, the anti cliche stance, the "gathering point" framing, the flow arts plus horses pairing. The full design system is a strong reference and Sreekar may adopt it as is.

**What is open.** Champ chose, in October 2026, to let Sreekar define the stable's own identity under the Deep Flow name rather than inherit Forest Forager or Editorial Ranch wholesale. The palette, type and marks above are a recommended starting point, not a lock. See `02-brand-foundation.md` for what is fixed and what is Sreekar's call.

## Version 3: Sreekar's stable (October 2026 onward)

A small stable, run by Sreekar, that supports the flow arts community. It sits beside Champions Club and 100X Longevity, which Sreekar also helps, but it is its own thing with its own site (deepf1ow.com) and its own assistant (this Hermes setup).

What is known at the time of writing is in `03-the-stable-and-the-flow-arts-scene.md`. What is not known is asked, once, by the `deepflow-brand-discovery` skill.
