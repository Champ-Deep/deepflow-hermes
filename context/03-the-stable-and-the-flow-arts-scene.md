# The stable and the flow arts scene

What is known, what is assumed, what is open. Hermes fills the open items by asking Sreekar through the `deepflow-brand-discovery` skill, one short session, then writes the answers back here.

## The stable (known)

- Run by Sreekar Surapaneni, Champ's brother. His personal project.
- Small. A few horses, not a commercial riding school. The point is a place where the flow arts crowd and the horse crowd overlap.
- Purpose: support the flow arts community. Space to practice, sessions, the occasional showcase or fire night, and a reason for people to come out of the city.
- Near Bangalore. Champions Ranch, the family group's 75 acre equestrian and corporate retreat property, is near Sarjapur in east Bangalore. Whether Sreekar's stable is on the Ranch, next to it, or somewhere else is open (question 2 below).
- Site: deepf1ow.com (see `07-domain-site-and-stack.md`).

## The stable (open, ask Sreekar once)

1. Does the stable have its own name, or is it "Deep Flow" full stop?
2. Where is it exactly, and is it on or beside Champions Ranch? This decides whether corporate groups come through Ranch or through Deep Flow.
3. What runs first: open practice evenings, intro workshops, rides, a monthly showcase, something else?
4. How many people can it host at once, and on which days?
5. Pricing posture: free open sessions with paid workshops, membership, pay per session, or "pay what it is worth"?
6. Instagram handle, and whether an account already exists.
7. Who else is involved (an instructor, a stable hand, a photographer friend)?
8. What does Sreekar personally practice, and what does he want to be known for?
9. What is the one thing he wants to be true in six months (for example "forty regulars who show up every Sunday")?
10. What must never happen (for example "no commercial riding lessons for kids", "no sponsor logos on the fence")?

## The flow arts scene in Bangalore (research from June 2026, verify before relying on it)

- **IndieFlow** is the Bangalore flow arts organization (founders Beri and Yacobeh). They run intro workshops in poi, hula hoop and slacklining for groups, and were shortlisted in June 2026 as the first partner to approach for Ranch workshops and evening fire or LED performances. Treat them as the natural first partner for Deep Flow too, and as people to collaborate with, not compete against.
- Flow arts in India sit mostly on Instagram and WhatsApp groups. Discovery happens through reels of practice sessions and festival clips, not through websites. The site's job is to be the place a reel points to.
- Props people use: poi, staff and dragon staff, hoops, fans, levi wand, rope dart, buugeng, fire variants of all of these. Fire needs a safety person, a fuel station and permission from the venue owner. At the stable, horses and fire do not share a paddock.
- Adjacent scenes that overlap: capoeira, contact juggling, acro yoga, slacklining, ecstatic dance, drum circles. These are where new flow artists come from.
- Ranch activity partner shortlist (June 2026) for reference: Kings Farm Equestrian (equine assisted learning, Bangalore), Pragalbha Yoga and Ujjayi Yoga (Sarjapur Road), Satvat Holistics and Sound Sutraa (sound healing, recovery), IndieFlow (flow arts). These were sourced for Champions Ranch's corporate add on menu, not for Deep Flow, but the same people will know Sreekar's audience.

## Event ladder (from version 1, still sound)

Start with the first two. Add the rest only when the first two have regulars.

| Tier | Format | Why first or later |
|---|---|---|
| First | Open practice evening (weekly, same day every week) | Cheapest to run, builds the habit, produces content for free |
| First | Intro workshop (monthly, one prop, beginners) | The on ramp. Most new people arrive here |
| Next | Showcase or fire night (quarterly) | The reason people bring friends. Needs a safety plan |
| Next | Ride and spin morning (horses plus flow, small groups) | The thing nobody else can offer |
| Later | Jam sessions, newbie nights, collab residencies, pop up market | Only with regulars and at least one helper |
| Via Ranch | Corporate groups, leadership programs, retreats | Routed through Champions Ranch and its intake form |
