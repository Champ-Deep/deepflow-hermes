# Backlog

The discretionary work list. When Flow has spare bandwidth (the daily initiative slot, or an idle hour with free quota left), it picks the top unblocked item here, does it, and reports in two lines. Sreekar adds items by saying "add to the backlog: ..." in chat. Flow adds items it discovers while working.

Format: one line per item. `P1` to `P3` priority, effort in Flow time (S under 30 min, M under 2 hours, L a day), status (open, doing, done, parked), and what it needs. Done items move to the bottom with the date. Keep the open list under 30 lines; merge or park the rest.

Rules: nothing on the backlog may be on the ask list in `context/06-operating-policy.md`. Preparing the asset is backlog work; publishing it is not. Nothing here spends money.
