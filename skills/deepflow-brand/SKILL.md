# Deep Flow brand

Source of truth: `context/02-brand-foundation.md` in the repo. This skill is the short form that loads every session. When the two differ, the context file wins and this skill gets fixed.

## When to use

Any caption, post, email, page, image prompt, event listing, bio, reply to a stranger, or partner message. Also when judging someone else's draft.

## Quick reference

**Name.** Deep Flow. Two words in prose. "deepflow" only as a handle or wordmark.

**Promise.** Flow state of mind to the grind state of life.

**One line.** A gathering point for people who practice movement and flow arts, anchored by a small stable near Bangalore.

**Not.** A wellness brand, a gym, a yoga studio, a productivity hack, a corporate venue (that is Champions Ranch).

**Audience.** Consumers in Bangalore. The person who saw a reel of a poi trail at golden hour and wants to know when the next session is. Riders and people who want to be around horses. Friends of regulars.

**Voice.** Wry but warm. Confident because the thing has been done. Specific to this stable. Short sentences. Say the time, the place and what to bring.

**Allowed words.** Flow, presence, state, motion, grounded, edge, build, ride, craft, earned.

**Banned.** Submit, Register Now, Sign up (as CTAs). Wellness journey, transformative, unlock, empower, elevate, holistic, mindful (as labels), tribe, vibe, community (as a buzzword), and the full no-ai-slop banned list. Emoji: one per post at most, none in headings or email. Em dashes and en dashes: none, anywhere.

**CTAs that work.** Come in. Reserve your spot. Save me a place. Ride with us. See the next session.

**Visual defaults until Sreekar decides** (see the table in `context/02-brand-foundation.md`): Pine `#1F4A36`, Cream `#F0E8D5`, Lilac `#D7CFEC`, Terracotta `#B95A2D`, Char `#1A1A14`. Pine holds the canvas, cream is the ground, lilac is the surprise, terracotta at most three times per view. Display Instrument Serif (GT Alpina if bought), body Inter, captions JetBrains Mono. No logo; small hand drawn marks (horse, poi, sun, leaf, sprig). No mascot.

**Photography.** Golden hour, bodies in motion or just after, dust in light, unstaged, never stock. Text on photos only with a solid band or a 60 to 70 percent pine overlay.

## Procedure

1. Name the audience and the channel in one line before drafting.
2. Draft in the voice. Put the time, place, cost and what to bring in the first two lines of any event piece.
3. Run the three tests: swap test (could another stable use it unchanged?), read aloud test (would Sreekar say it to a friend?), golden hour test (does the visual feel like 6 pm light on open ground?).
4. Run `no-ai-slop` in gate mode.
5. Search the output for the two dash characters. Zero.

## Pitfalls

- Writing about "the community" instead of about people, sessions and horses.
- Borrowing Champions Ranch's corporate tone or selling corporate packages in Deep Flow's voice.
- Longevity or health claims. None, ever, without Sreekar's own words and a source.
- Three hashtags maximum. No hashtag walls.
- Inventing a session, a price or a date to make copy feel real. Use placeholders in square brackets and ask.

## Verification

Before handing anything back: audience named, first two lines carry time or place or offer, no banned words, no dashes, no claims without a source, one CTA.
